Your data stays yours

Your library lives on your computer.

CiteSavant keeps your library in your browser, on your own computer. There’s no account and no server, and nothing is uploaded.

Because it lives in your browser, keep a safe copy: save your library to a folder on your disk (in Chrome or Edge), or download a backup file.

The one thing CiteSavant ever sends is a DOI number to Crossref, the public catalogue of published work, and only when you press “Look up”.

If AI features arrive and you choose to use them, what you send goes to the AI provider you picked, only when you press send.
