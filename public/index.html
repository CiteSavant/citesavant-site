Why I made it

I make small tools that help people learn by doing the thinking themselves. CiteSavant will never write for you. Any AI it offers will help you find and check, and leave the thinking to you.

It gives you a good place to read closely, keep what you find, and use it in your own words. Each tool I make is built so that you own your data. What you put into CiteSavant stays in your browser, on your computer. It never reaches me, because there is no server of mine for it to go to. And the tools are free.

Ian O’Neil, creator of CiteSavant

How it works

Three steps, in the order you already work

  1. Add a source

    Drop in a PDF or a Word document. It stays on your computer.

  2. Highlight what matters

    Select a passage and keep it. Give it a tag or a note while it’s fresh.

  3. Write

    Drag a highlight into your project to quote or cite it. The list of sources follows.

Key features

Three places, one path

Sources go into your Library. What you learn from them becomes Knowledge. Knowledge goes into your Projects, and every citation leads back to its page.

Add PDF and Word documents, search every word inside them, and sort them into collections you define.

Each file is kept once, exactly as it came, so every citation leads back to the real page.
